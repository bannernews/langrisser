Iron Crystal Sphere
Summons a storm sphere, moving all enemies 2 tiles in the selected direction. Then, all allies gain "Tailwind": Mobility +2; when entering combat while under the direction of Tailwind, ATK and INT +10%. Ignores immunity, cannot be dispelled and expires in the next round.
Sphere of the Wind Crystal
Increases the wearer's Mobility by 2 for 2 turns and grants "Blessing of the Wind Knot": before actively entering combat, damage increases by 6% for every tile moved, up to a maximum of 42%. After combat, the unit can move an additional 2 tiles. Lasts 2 turns. Cannot be dispelled. Ignores immunity.
Sphere of Sand-Mist
Summon a sphere. All enemies have their mobility reduced by 2 and cannot use the "Guard" effect. All allies gain the "Spiked Blade" effect: before combat, deals fixed damage equal to 2x (ATK + INT) to all enemies within a 2-tile radius. It ignores immunity, cannot be dispelled and lasts for 1 turn.
Sphere of Sands of Repose
Grants the wearer "Nobility of the Silent Sands": At the start of battle, applies "Decrepitude" to the enemy, increasing fixed damage taken by 100%. Lasts 1 turn. Ignores immunity. Cannot be dispelled. Additionally, at the start of battle, damage dealt increases by 30%; for every additional block from the enemy, damage dealt decreases by 15%, up to a maximum reduction of 30%. Lasts 2 turns. Ignores immunity. Cannot be dispelled.
Settled Snow Crystal
Summon a sphere. Selects 1 tile to apply the "Snowy Hill + Frozen Portal + Icy Docks" terrain effect. The effect also applies to all tiles within a 2-tile radius. It lasts until the next turn. Allies on this terrain gain +1 Skill Range. When initiating an attack and dealing damage they ignore 15% of the enemy's DEF and MDEF. All stats except HP increase by 10%.
Crystal of Ice and Snow
Grants the bearer "Blessing of Ice and Snow": Before dealing active damage, applies 1 random debuff to the enemy. When initiating combat, deals fixed damage equal to 0.1x the damage dealt to the target and enemies within a 1-tile radius. Increases the unit's critical hit rate by 50%. Ignores immunity. Cannot be dispelled. Expires in the next round.
Crystal Sphere of Thunderous Strike
Summons a sphere. Gains a "Static Charge" for each enemy hit by the damage. For every 3/5/8 stacks of "Static Charge" at the end of the turn it unleashes 2/4/6 "Thunderstrikes" against random enemies anywhere on the map, dealing fixed damage equal to 2x the Orb's (ATK + INT). Damage dealt to the same enemy increases by 20% with each successive hit. Each time an enemy is struck by a "Thunderstrike," they gain 1 stack of "Quick Temper," increasing their chance of receiving a critical hit by 50%; this effect stacks up to 2 times and lasts for 2 turns. Ignores immunity. Cannot be dispelled.
Sphere of the Drizzling Crystal
Grants the bearer "Dew's Favor": Skill damage increases by 20%. When dealing skill damage, if a critical hit occurs, the skill's cooldown is reduced by 2. Lasts 2 turns. Ignores immunity. Cannot be dispelled.
